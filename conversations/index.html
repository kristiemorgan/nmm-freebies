Staying compliant on Instagram and Facebook
  • Spread your prospecting across the day. Don't do it all in one block.
  • Never send the identical message to lots of people. Change up your opener, your follow message and your direct message.
  • Don't blast 50 people a day. Aim for 10 to 20 real conversations a day in total, from your new follows, ads, story watchers and people engaging with your content.
  • Using ManyChat? Set a 12 hour delay between someone following you and the message going out, and load in multiple message variations.
The pink [brackets]
  • Anything in pink brackets is yours to swap out before you send. Use their name, their words and their goals, exactly the way they said them.

Step 01

Start the conversation

Every conversation starts one of these ways. Find the entry point that matches how they came to you, use those messages, then move to Step 02.

Before you reach out

Before sending a direct message to someone cold, warm the profile up first. This is the 2:1 engagement method.

Like 1 post
Comment on 1 recent post with something meaningful
Engage with a recent story
Then follow

They followed you: the follow message

Change your openers upRotate through these rather than using the same one every time. You'll see what converts better, you'll see what creates more real conversations, and you won't get flagged.
The structure that works best"Are you here for this, or that?" Giving them two doors to walk through opens up the next part of the conversation. A question they can answer with one word usually ends it.
Be specific about your contentDon't say "the content and tips I share." Say "the content and tips I share about [what you actually talk about]." When they answer "motherhood," you've got your hook straight into a conversation.

Objections

Don't handle real objections over DM. Acknowledge what they've said, then move the conversation to a call. Trying to win an objection over text is how conversations die.

They say"I'm good for now."
Reply

Totally fine. If anything changes or you want to explore options later, I'm here and happy to chat when timing feels right.

They say"Is this MLM?"
Reply

Great question. [Explain simply how your business works.] Can I ask what your experience with MLM has been? Most people ask because they've had a bad experience before.

Alternate reply

That's probably the question I get asked the most. It's very different from what most people picture. Happy to explain how it actually works if you're curious?

They say"Tell me more."
Reply

Absolutely. Rather than trying to explain it over dozens of messages, I have a short overview that walks through everything clearly. If it makes sense afterwards, we can chat. If it doesn't, no pressure at all. Will you be able to set aside [length of video] today?

Reply

Honestly, I'd be more concerned if you weren't. Most people who end up doing well started off asking lots of questions. May I ask… what's the biggest thing you're sceptical about?

I totally get that, being a little sceptical is healthy. Would it help if we jumped on a quick call so you can ask your questions and see if it actually fits your goals?
